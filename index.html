How to play

Everything you need to hold the road.

🎯 Goal: stop enemies reaching your blue base. Lose all ❤️ and it's over.
🏗️ Build: pick a tower (keys 1–9, 0), click a green tile. Hover shows its range — green = OK, red = blocked.
Mud: paint it on the road to slow enemies to 55% speed — perfect kill-zone ground. Slow-immune brutes plow through. Water, Forest & Rock are decorative blockers: no building, no walking, just looks.
🌊 Waves: press Space to send the next wave. Clearing waves earns bonus gold.
⬆️ Upgrade: click a placed tower, then Upgrade U or Sell X. Esc deselects.
🏆 Campaign: 120 maps across 6 worlds, each on Easy / Normal / Hard. Pick a world, then a level; finishing a map unlocks the next, and finishing map 20 unlocks the next world. Stars (★) track your best difficulty per map — saved in your browser.
🎥 Views: everything plays in cinematic 3D. Drag orbits, right-drag / Shift-drag pans, wheel / pinch zooms, click places and selects, 🎯 / C recenters. In the map editor, drag paints tiles — hold Space while dragging to orbit instead.
